ShareWave Privacy Policy

Operator: CyberAlvi · Effective date: November 9, 2026 · App: ShareWave for Android (com.cyberalvi.sharewave)

What ShareWave does

ShareWave lets you choose files to transfer directly between devices on a local network. It does not require an account, and it does not use a ShareWave cloud service, advertising, or analytics. When you use WaveWeb, a browser on the same network can exchange selected files with your device after you authorize the browser.

CyberAlvi does not receive, collect or sell your files or personal data through the app.

Information handled by the app

  • Files and transfer details. ShareWave accesses the files you select for sending and writes files you agree to receive. To manage transfers and their resumption, it stores information on your device such as file names, paths or file references, sizes, transfer status, times, and the name, identifier and last known network address of the other device. The contents and names of your files may themselves contain personal information.
  • Device discovery and pairing. When discovery or receive mode is active, ShareWave sends discovery messages on the local network. These messages can include a device identifier, device name, platform and port. They are not encrypted or authenticated, so other devices on the same network may observe them. Your device name may be based on the name configured on your phone or its manufacturer and model; you can change it in the app. ShareWave also stores information about paired devices on your device.
  • Camera. If you choose to scan a connection QR code, ShareWave asks for camera access and uses it only to read the code. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Preferences and notifications. ShareWave stores your settings locally. With your permission where required by Android, it displays notifications for features such as active transfers.

ShareWave transfers the files you select to the devices or browser sessions you authorize. Choose your recipients carefully: recipients can retain or share the files they receive. ShareWave does not send these files to a ShareWave server.

Security

Device-to-device transfers use encrypted TLS connections and device authentication. A new device is paired using a QR code or a matching six-digit code. Discovery messages, however, are separate from the encrypted transfer connection and are visible on the local network.

WaveWeb uses HTTPS with a self-signed certificate. Your browser may show a certificate warning; compare the certificate fingerprint shown by the browser with the fingerprint shown by ShareWave before proceeding. Authorize a browser only when the six-digit codes match. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Storage and deletion

Transfer history, paired-device information, settings and app identity are stored only on your device. You can clear transfer history in the app; clearing history does not automatically delete received files. Depending on the entry, the app also offers an option to delete associated received files. You can manage received files using your device's file manager or the destination you selected.

Received files are normally saved in Download/ShareWave or in a folder you choose. Uninstalling ShareWave removes its app-private data and device identity, but files saved in shared storage or a chosen folder can remain on your device. Delete those files separately if you no longer want them. Other recipients control copies received on their devices. Temporary partial transfers are cleaned up after they become old, subject to Android scheduling.

Children

ShareWave is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
